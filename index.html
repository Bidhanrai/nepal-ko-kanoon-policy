Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Finnove Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us", "our"), the operator of the Nepalko Kanoon mobile application and website (the "App"), handles information in connection with your use of the App. We built Nepalko Kanoon as a free public tool to help people understand legal fees and taxes in Nepal — and we collect as little personal information as possible.

1. Summary

  • The App's calculators work without you creating an account or giving us your name.
  • The figures you enter (e.g. a claim amount or property value) are used to calculate your result and are not used to identify you.
  • We use standard analytics to understand how the App is used, and this may involve third-party services (e.g. Google).
  • If you choose to contact a partner (e.g. via WhatsApp), the details you send are shared with that partner because you initiated it.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you enter into calculators

To produce a result, the App asks for inputs such as a disputed/claim amount, authorized or paid-up capital, property value, location type, or dates. Treat these as calculation inputs, not identity data. Where possible, these are processed on your device or transiently to return your result. We do not require your name, address, or contact details to use the calculators.

2.2 Information collected automatically

Like most apps, we may collect limited technical and usage data automatically, such as device type, operating system, app version, language setting, general (city/region-level) location inferred from your device or network, pages/tools used, and crash/diagnostic data. This helps us keep the App working and improve it.

2.3 Information you provide when contacting a partner

Some tools offer an optional handoff to a partner service via WhatsApp or a similar channel. If you tap that option, a message may be pre-filled with the inputs you provided (such as company type or transaction value) so the partner can help you. Sending that message is your choice, and the information then travels to the partner under their privacy practices.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the calculators and return your results.
  • To operate, maintain, secure, and improve the App.
  • To understand aggregate usage (which tools are used, in which language) so we can prioritise improvements.
  • To connect you with a partner service when — and only when — you choose that option.
  • To comply with law and enforce our Terms.

We do not use the amounts you enter to build a personal profile of you, and we do not sell personal information.

4. Sharing & disclosure

We share information only as described here:

  • Service providers who help us run the App (e.g. hosting, analytics, crash reporting) under contractual confidentiality.
  • Partners you choose to contact — when you tap a handoff (e.g. WhatsApp), the information you send goes to them at your initiation.
  • Legal & safety — where required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security.
  • Business transfers — in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, with notice as required by law.

5. Third-party services

The App relies on third-party services that have their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to review. These may include Google Play services / Firebase / analytics provider for app functionality and analytics, and WhatsApp / Meta if you use a partner-contact feature. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third parties or of any partner you choose to engage.

6. Data retention

We keep automatically-collected technical and usage data only as long as needed for the purposes above, and then delete or anonymise it. Because the calculators don't require an account, we generally do not retain the specific inputs you type in a form tied to your identity.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

The App is intended for a general adult audience dealing with legal and tax matters and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

9. Your choices & rights

  • You can use the calculators without an account.
  • You can control device permissions (e.g. approximate location) and analytics through your device settings.
  • Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you by contacting us.

10. International users

The App is operated from Nepal and intended primarily for matters under Nepali law. If you access it from elsewhere, you do so on your own initiative and are responsible for local compliance.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, provide additional notice where appropriate. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact us
