1. Overview

Finnove Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("Finnove," "we," "us," "our") built नेपालको कानून, a free tool that helps people estimate Nepali government fees — currently court filing fees, with company registration and property transfer fee estimators planned. This policy covers the mobile app only.

2. Information we collect

Calculation inputs stay on your device. Case type, court level, case stage, disputed amount, and any other figures you enter into a calculator are processed entirely on your device. We do not transmit these details, or the results, to our servers.

Automatically collected technical and usage data. We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (both Google products) to understand how the app is used and to catch bugs. This can include:

  • Device model, operating system, and app version
  • General location at the country or region level, derived from IP address — not precise GPS
  • App usage events, such as which tool or screen was opened and session length
  • If the app crashes: a diagnostic log with the stack trace and device state (memory, storage, OS version) at the time

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any account credentials — the app has no user accounts.

Feedback you choose to send. The "Feedback" option in Settings opens your own email app, addressed to admin@finnovetech.com. Anything you write is delivered by your email provider; the app itself does not collect or transmit it.

3. How we use information

To keep the app stable (crash diagnostics), to understand which tools are useful and improve them, and to respond to feedback you choose to send us.

4. Third-party services

We use Firebase (a Google product) for analytics and crash reporting. Firebase processes data on our behalf under Google's own terms and practices — see Google's Privacy Policy. The app is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play Store, which have their own separate privacy practices governing your relationship with them as platforms.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell your data. Information collected through Firebase is shared only with Google as our service provider, as described above. We do not share data with any other third party.

6. Data retention

Analytics and crash data are retained only as long as necessary for the purposes described above, in line with Firebase's data retention settings for our project.

7. Children's privacy

नेपालको कानून is built for general adult use — helping with court cases, business registration, and property matters — and is not directed at, or intended for use by, children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us at admin@finnovetech.com and we'll remove it.

8. Your choices

You can limit analytics collection through your device's privacy settings (for example, Android's ad personalization controls or iOS's tracking permissions). You can also contact us to ask what information, if any, is associated with your device.

9. Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the app, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves — for example, if we introduce user accounts in the future. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do, and note material changes in the app's store listing update notes.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email admin@finnovetech.com.
